# Audit Finding AF-2026-019

- **Engagement:** FY26 data-handling control review
- **Date:** 2026-02-19
- **Site:** Manchester service desk
- **Retired standard referenced:** Halbrook Data Handling Standard, Edition 3.1 (retired 31 December 2025)

## Finding

The document reviewed cites the retired provision below:

> Logs must be protected against modification and retained for at least twelve (12) months.

Cited provision: **Clause 12(2)**

**Reviewer observation:** The service desk procedure reproduces the wording above. The reviewer noted only a difference in clause numbering on the procedure leaflet.
