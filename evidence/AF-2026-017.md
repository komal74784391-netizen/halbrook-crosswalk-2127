# Audit Finding AF-2026-017

- **Engagement:** FY26 data-handling control review
- **Date:** 2026-02-17
- **Site:** Dublin backup facility
- **Retired standard referenced:** Halbrook Data Handling Standard, Edition 3.1 (retired 31 December 2025)

## Finding

The document reviewed cites the retired provision below:

> Backups of Restricted data must be retained for a minimum of thirty-five (35) days.

Cited provision: **Clause 8(1)**

**Reviewer observation:** The facility runbook still uses the retention period quoted above. The runbook owner has been asked to schedule an update.
