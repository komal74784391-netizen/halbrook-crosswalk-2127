# Audit Finding AF-2026-011

- **Engagement:** FY26 data-handling control review
- **Date:** 2026-02-11
- **Site:** Frankfurt shared-service centre
- **Retired standard referenced:** Halbrook Data Handling Standard, Edition 3.1 (retired 31 December 2025)

## Finding

The document reviewed cites the retired provision below:

> This Standard applies to any third-party processor acting on the Company's behalf, including processors engaged under a trial or pilot agreement.

Cited provision: **Clause 4(2)**

**Reviewer observation:** The operating procedure at the Frankfurt site still reproduces the wording shown above. The procedure has not been refreshed since the edition change.
