# Audit Finding AF-2026-023

- **Engagement:** FY26 data-handling control review
- **Date:** 2026-02-23
- **Site:** Toronto engineering
- **Retired standard referenced:** Halbrook Data Handling Standard, Edition 3.1 (retired 31 December 2025)

## Finding

The document reviewed cites the retired provision below:

> Access to Company Data is granted only to individuals with a documented business need and is limited to the minimum scope required to perform the assigned duties.

Cited provision: **Clause 3(1)**

**Reviewer observation:** The engineering handbook reproduces the retired sentence above. The handbook's section numbering no longer matches the standard.
