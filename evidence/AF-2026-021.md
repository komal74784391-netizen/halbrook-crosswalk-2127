# Audit Finding AF-2026-021

- **Engagement:** FY26 data-handling control review
- **Date:** 2026-02-21
- **Site:** Privacy Office, London
- **Retired standard referenced:** Halbrook Data Handling Standard, Edition 3.1 (retired 31 December 2025)

## Finding

The document reviewed cites the retired provision below:

> The Privacy Office must acknowledge a data subject request within ten (10) business days of receipt and fulfil it within thirty (30) calendar days of receipt.

Cited provision: **Clause 10(3)**

**Reviewer observation:** The Privacy Office operating manual quotes the retired fulfilment rule above. The manual predates the current edition.
